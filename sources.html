Primary railway references used for measured values, plus the boundaries of what this build plan can claim as official.

Official railway references

  • RDSO locomotive pneumatic/rolling-stock specification, RDSO/2017/EL/SPEC/0126 Rev. 0. Lists WAP-7 length 20,562 mm, six-axle data and locomotive/coach operating parameters. Open RDSO specification ↗
  • RDSO LHB AC coach specification, RDSO/2013/CG/B/01. Gives LHB body length 23,540 mm, length over buffers 24,000 mm, width 3,240 mm, height 4,039 mm, floor height and variant tables. Open RDSO LHB specification ↗
  • RDSO revised LHB maintenance manual, Volume II, Chapter I. Lists AC three-tier coach capacity and dimensions, including 72 berths for the described EOG LHB variant. Open LHB manual ↗
  • CLW Driver's Manual for WAG-9 / WAG-9H / WAP-7 family. Used for common platform dimensions such as 3,152 mm width, 4,255 mm maximum locked-pantograph height, 12,000 mm bogie centres and wheel dimensions. The accessible copy is hosted by a third party; verify against an official CLW-issued copy before engineering use. Open archived manual copy ↗
  • Indian Railways Schedule of Dimensions 2022, Railway Board. Governing broad-gauge infrastructure reference; the Railway Board page lists current correction slips. Open Railway Board IRSOD page ↗
  • RDSO rolling-stock specification. States high passenger platform height range 760–840 mm and Mumbai/Pune suburban exceptions. Open RDSO platform reference ↗
  • RDSO web drawing list. Lists coach layout and profile drawing identifiers; use the exact approved GA drawing for the chosen AC 3-tier coach before modeling internal dimensions. Open RDSO drawing list ↗

What is not an official measurement?

Project conversion

1 stud = 0.28 m is the current project convention from your existing reference diagrams. Stud counts shown on the train page are calculated from real measurements. They are not railway-issued values.

Custom game plan

Central Junction, its 15 platforms and their lengths, the 8 m platform width, the five-city corridor sketch and its station arrangement are custom game-world proposals. No real terminal's approved site drawings were supplied.

Important for model accuracy: dimensions shown here cover the published outer envelope and selected mechanical references. A complete 1:1 interior requires the exact variant's released general-arrangement and equipment drawings. The HTML interior sketch is explicitly conceptual, not measured.
