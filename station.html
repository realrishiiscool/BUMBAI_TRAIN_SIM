A fictional Mumbai-inspired terminus based on the existing project brief. This page separates real railway standards from the custom station dimensions you can build in-game.

Verified reference: Indian Railways high passenger platforms are generally 760–840 mm above rail level; the Mumbai suburban exception can be higher. For this long-distance terminus plan, use 760 mm as the baseline, then validate coach-step clearance. Width is not one universal fixed value; plan it for passenger flow and site conditions.

Station geometry & facilities

Track arrangement

  • 15 terminal platform tracks with buffer stops at the dead end.
  • Station throat joins the platform tracks to the up/down main lines.
  • Depot lead branches from the mainline to a 3-track inspection shed and parking sidings.
  • Concourse and player spawn at the buffer-stop end; footbridge or concourse path to every platform.
  • Provide signals before switches and a visible route indicator for each platform path.

Buildable station kit

  • Platform deck, coping edge, tactile strip, ramped end, drains and accessible route.
  • Modular canopy bays, columns, roof trusses, lights, fans and rain gutters.
  • Number signs, departure boards, clock, benches, bins, water point and help desk.
  • Ticket hall, waiting room, toilets, vendor stalls, station office, staff and passenger NPC zones.
  • Station throat, crossovers, buffer stops, overhead electrification and safety fencing.
What is a real reference and what is my made-up station?

The platform-height reference is from RDSO/Indian Railways material. The 15 platforms, 8 m width, specific lengths, building arrangement, station name and schematic track ladder are custom Bumbai Train Sim design decisions. A real station's exact drawings would need a named real terminal and its approved engineering plans.
