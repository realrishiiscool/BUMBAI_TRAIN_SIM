Four journeys.

Mumbai is the starting hub. From Central Junction, the game branches north toward Agra and Delhi, and southeast toward Secunderabad and Visakhapatnam.

Five-city route layout

Build the world as five regions

Hub / Region 01

Mumbai

Central Junction, depot, dense suburbs, coast-side skyline and the starter route.

North / Region 02

Agra

Mid-route stop with a major landmark silhouette and a smaller terminal zone.

North / Region 03

Delhi

Large city destination, interchange station, yard and long-distance service terminal.

South-east / Region 04

Secunderabad

Rail junction region with split routes, service sidings and a distinct dry-city palette.

Coast / Region 05

Visakhapatnam

Coastal endpoint with port/industrial scenery and a passenger terminal.

Route construction plan

PASS 1Mumbai ↔ DepotFirst 1–2 km playable trip.
PASS 2Mumbai ↔ AgraBuild north route modules and stations.
PASS 3Agra ↔ DelhiComplete the northern corridor.
PASS 4Secunderabad ↔ VizagAdd the southeast branch after the hub loop.

To preserve realistic geography at 1:1, these are enormous distances. A practical game may use compressed travel segments between detailed 1:1 city zones. Keep the station/train assets 1:1 and choose an explicit route compression only for long empty stretches.
